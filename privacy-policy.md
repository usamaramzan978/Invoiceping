# Privacy Policy for InvoicePing

**Last Updated:** January 18, 2026

## 1. Introduction

InvoicePing ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our invoicing and payment reminder service, including our integration with WhatsApp Business API, email services, and SMS services.

By using InvoicePing, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Account Information
- Name and email address
- Business profile information (company name, address, contact details)
- Authentication credentials (encrypted passwords)

### 2.2 Invoice and Business Data
- Client information (names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses)
- Invoice details (amounts, items, due dates, payment status)
- Payment records and transaction history
- Business profile and branding information

### 2.3 Communication Data
- Message content sent via WhatsApp, email, or SMS
- Recipient contact information (phone numbers, email addresses)
- Message delivery status and logs
- Template preferences and customizations

### 2.4 Technical Data
- IP addresses
- Browser type and version
- Device information
- Usage patterns and analytics
- Log files and error reports

### 2.5 WhatsApp Integration Data
- WhatsApp Business API access tokens (securely stored)
- Phone number IDs and business account information
- Message delivery confirmations
- WhatsApp message metadata

### 3.1 Service Provision

### 3.2 Communication
- Sending invoices and payment reminders to your clients

### 3.3 Service Improvement
- Analyzing usage patterns to improve our services

### 3.4 Legal Compliance
- Complying with applicable laws and regulations

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

### 4.1 Third-Party Services
We use the following third-party services that may process your data:

**WhatsApp Business API (Meta)**
- We use WhatsApp Business API to send payment reminders and invoice notifications
- Messages are sent through Meta's infrastructure
- Meta's Privacy Policy applies to WhatsApp communications: https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy
- We do not share your data with Meta beyond what is necessary for message delivery

**Email Service Providers**
- We use email service providers to send invoices and reminders
- Email providers process recipient email addresses and message content
- Standard email delivery protocols are used

**SMS Service Providers**
- We use SMS service providers to send text message reminders
- Phone numbers and message content are shared with SMS providers
- SMS providers are bound by their own privacy policies

**Cloud Hosting and Infrastructure**
- Your data is stored on secure cloud servers
- Hosting providers have access to encrypted data for infrastructure management
- All hosting providers are bound by strict data protection agreements

### 4.2 We Do NOT
- Sell your personal information to third parties
- Share your data with advertisers or marketing companies
- Use your data for purposes other than providing our service
- Access your WhatsApp conversations beyond what is necessary for service delivery

### 4.3 Legal Requirements
We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government regulation, or if we believe disclosure is necessary to:
- Protect our rights, property, or safety
- Protect the rights, property, or safety of our users
- Investigate fraud or security issues
- Comply with legal obligations

## 7. WhatsApp Business API Specific Information

### 7.1 Message Processing
- We use WhatsApp Business API to send invoice reminders and payment notifications
- Messages are sent on your behalf to your clients
- We do not read or store your clients' responses unless you explicitly enable two-way messaging
- Message content is generated from your invoice data and templates

### 7.2 Data Sharing with Meta
- We share necessary information (phone numbers, message content) with Meta to deliver messages
- Meta processes this data according to their Privacy Policy
- We do not share your business data or client lists with Meta beyond message delivery

### 7.3 Client Consent
- You are responsible for obtaining consent from your clients before sending them WhatsApp messages
- You must comply with WhatsApp's Business Policy and anti-spam requirements
- We provide tools to manage opt-outs and unsubscribe requests

### 7.4 Message Logs
- We log message delivery status for service improvement
- Logs include recipient phone numbers, delivery status, and timestamps
- Logs are retained for a reasonable period for troubleshooting and compliance

## 8. Data Retention

### 8.1 Active Accounts
- We retain your data as long as your account is active
- Inactive accounts may be archived after a period of inactivity
- You will be notified before account deletion

### 8.2 Deleted Accounts
- Upon account deletion, most data is permanently deleted within 30 days
- Some data may be retained longer for legal compliance or dispute resolution
- Backup copies may be retained for up to 90 days

### 8.3 Legal Requirements
- We may retain certain data longer if required by law
- Financial records may be retained for tax and accounting purposes
- Legal hold may apply to data relevant to ongoing disputes
